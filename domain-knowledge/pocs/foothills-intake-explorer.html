Who this helps, and the question it answers

Supply planners

If next year runs dry, what water does the plant get?

Panels put a drought year (2026) beside wetter ones at the same gauge. Planners see the quality side of the flows they already forecast.

Water Quality & Treatment

Does the spring TOC pulse follow rising flow, and which gauge sees it first?

Connected days trace each year's path. Switch gauges and lag to find where the signal shows up earliest.

Plant operators

At today's flow, how close have we been to the 60 mg/L line?

Set the what-if flow to today's reading and read the range each past year delivered.

What this cannot tell you yet

  • Five water years of plant data. It describes patterns; it does not predict.
  • Most gauges sit below reservoirs, so flow there reflects releases. The two headwater creeks are the closest to natural runoff here.
  • Denver Water changed sources in 2026. Some of the 2026 difference may come from that, not the river.
  • The 2-day default lag follows Jake's Sep 4 update; he asks that the exact number not be leaned on.
